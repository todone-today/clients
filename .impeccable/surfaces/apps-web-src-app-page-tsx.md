# Landing page (/) — surface brief

Scope: the public landing page at todone.today. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: people and small teams/households, many in India, choosing a todo app; open-source folks; potential supporters.
Job: understand what todone does today, why it's different (direct manipulation, open source, your data), and the long-term ecosystem (web → mobile → e-ink). Actions: Start a list (primary), Try the demo, support mobile funding, contribute on GitHub.
Proof: the live board itself (real components), real screenshots via an image config the user will fill, honest roadmap, $124 funding target with real progress only (members' purchases; starts at $0; section hides when reached), prices marked "opening soon" until Razorpay is live.
Constraints: no invented users, testimonials or numbers; hardware shown as exploratory concepts with labelled placeholders; keep "Lists that actually get done."

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is a working todone board. The category's centred headline over feature cards is refused: the first screen hands you stickers to drag onto real notes, and every section below is one sticker peeled into its feature.

OWN-WORLD: The sticker world from DESIGN.md at full commitment: flat lime, butter, pink, cyan, peri and orange fills, black keylines, hard offset shadows, slight tilt, Big Shoulders display, Schibsted Grotesk body, cream/near-black ground. Stickers are the only control vocabulary: chips, buttons and section labels are stickers.

STORY: See a board, drag "Tomorrow" onto a note and watch it change; learn steps, the week, sharing, offline and integrations one peeled sticker at a time; see the roadmap as notes on the same board (now, next, someday), the funding target, the pricing deal, and how to join in.

Right: a vertical sticker sheet (Tomorrow, 1 hr, High, Weekly) with a hint "drag one onto a note". Under the headline note: Start a list (lime, primary) and Try the demo. Nav above: logo, Guide, Changelog, Status, GitHub, theme, Open the app.
